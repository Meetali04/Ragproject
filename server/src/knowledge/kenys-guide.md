# Kenya Travel Guide

## Overview

Kenya is a country in East Africa with varied landscapes, including the Indian Ocean coast, savannah, highlands, lakes, and semi-arid northern regions. Nairobi is the capital and the main international gateway for many visitors. Kenya has two official languages, English and Kiswahili (Swahili). The national currency is the Kenyan shilling (KES), and the country uses East Africa Time (UTC+3).

Travel times within Kenya can be long. Distances on a map do not always reflect journey time because road conditions, traffic, weather, and route conditions vary. Plan fewer stops rather than trying to cover the whole country in one trip.

## Regions and Places to Visit

### Nairobi

Nairobi is a large, busy city and a common starting or ending point for a Kenya trip. Visitors may combine city time with Nairobi National Park, museums, markets, restaurants, and day trips. Allow for city traffic when arranging airport transfers or connecting transport.

### Maasai Mara and the southwest

The Maasai Mara is a protected wildlife area in southwestern Kenya known for open grassland and wildlife viewing. It is part of a broader ecosystem that crosses the Kenya-Tanzania border. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Seasonal animal movements vary with rainfall, and no single month guarantees a particular sighting.

### Amboseli and the south

Amboseli National Park is in southern Kenya near the Tanzania border. It is known for views of Mount Kilimanjaro when weather and visibility allow, as well as elephant herds. Kilimanjaro is across the border in Tanzania; the mountain view is from Kenya.

### Tsavo

Tsavo East and Tsavo West are large national parks in southeastern Kenya. They are often considered together with the coast or a southern Kenya itinerary. Their size means drives between areas and wildlife-viewing locations can take significant time.

### Rift Valley lakes

The Rift Valley includes lakes and highland landscapes. Lake Nakuru National Park is known for wildlife, including rhinoceroses, while Lake Naivasha is a freshwater lake area used for nature activities. Water levels, wildlife conditions, and activity availability can change.

### Northern Kenya

Northern Kenya includes arid and semi-arid landscapes and distinct local cultures. Samburu National Reserve is one established wildlife destination in this region. Remote travel requires advance planning with experienced operators; road access, services, and communications may be limited in some areas.

### Indian Ocean coast

Kenya's coast offers beaches, marine activities, and Swahili cultural heritage. Diani Beach is a popular beach destination south of Mombasa. Lamu Old Town is a historic Swahili settlement with narrow streets and a distinctive built environment. Respect local customs, especially around religious sites and in smaller coastal communities.

## When to Visit

Kenya's climate differs by altitude and region. The coast is generally hot and humid, while Nairobi and the highlands are cooler. Northern areas are generally drier, but weather varies.

Kenya commonly has wetter periods around March to May and October to December, but rainfall timing and intensity vary by year and location. Rain can affect road access and wildlife viewing. Dry periods can make some wildlife easier to spot near water, but conditions differ between parks and sightings are never assured.

The Great Migration is a cross-border movement of wildebeest and other wildlife in the Serengeti-Mara ecosystem. Its timing depends on rainfall and grazing conditions. Do not promise an exact crossing date or a guaranteed sighting; check recent local reports and discuss flexible plans with a licensed operator.

Before choosing dates, check current weather forecasts, park notices, accommodation availability, and transport schedules. The seasons described here are broad patterns, not a forecast.

## Trip Length and Sample Routes

### Short trip: 4 to 5 days

Choose one main area, such as Nairobi with a nearby nature visit, or a single safari region. Avoid adding a distant coast or a second faraway park unless internal flights are part of the plan.

### Safari and city: 7 to 9 days

A practical outline is arrival and recovery time in Nairobi, several nights in one or two safari areas, and a final night near the departure airport. Confirm transfer times before booking connecting flights.

### Safari and coast: 10 to 14 days

Allow several nights for safari and several nights on the coast. Consider a domestic flight for a long transfer if it fits the budget and schedule. Keep buffer time before an international departure.

These are planning examples, not fixed itineraries. Actual journey times, park access, flight schedules, and traveler needs should determine the final route.

## Transport and Getting Around

- Nairobi is the main hub for many international and domestic connections.
- Road transfers are common for parks and towns. Ask the operator about realistic drive time, road conditions, stops, and vehicle type.
- Domestic flights can reduce long overland journeys, but luggage limits, schedules, and airstrip transfers vary by airline and lodge.
- The Nairobi-Mombasa railway is another option for some coast journeys. Check the current timetable, station transfer arrangements, and ticket rules before relying on it.
- In cities, use reputable, traceable transport and agree on pickup details. Avoid making assumptions about late-night transport availability.
- Build rest time into long routes. A route with fewer destinations is usually more comfortable than a tightly packed itinerary.

## Safari Planning and Wildlife Etiquette

- Book parks, conservancies, accommodation, and guides in advance during busy periods.
- Check whether park fees, conservation fees, meals, transfers, and game drives are included in a quoted price.
- Follow ranger and guide instructions. Stay inside the vehicle where required, keep a safe distance from animals, and never feed or call wildlife.
- Keep noise low and do not pressure guides to disturb animals for a photograph.
- Use binoculars or a camera zoom rather than approaching wildlife.
- Ask about conservation practices and community benefits when choosing a tour operator or conservancy stay.
- Park rules, fees, opening arrangements, and conservation-area access can change. Confirm them with the relevant authority or operator before travel.

## Culture and Respectful Travel

Kenya is culturally and linguistically diverse. Customs vary between communities, regions, and settings; do not assume that one custom represents everyone.

- Ask permission before photographing people, homes, ceremonies, or cultural sites.
- Dress modestly when visiting religious places and in communities where beachwear is not appropriate.
- A polite greeting is appreciated. English and Kiswahili are widely used, but language use varies by location.
- Ask before entering private or community land and follow local guidance.
- Buy crafts from responsible sellers and avoid products made from wildlife parts.
- When visiting a community or cultural attraction, choose experiences led by local people and ask how the visit benefits them.

## Practical Packing

- Pack light, breathable clothing and sun protection for warm lowland and coastal areas.
- Bring a warm layer for early-morning game drives and cooler highland evenings.
- Carry comfortable closed shoes for walking, plus suitable footwear for beach activities if relevant.
- Bring insect repellent, personal medication, a reusable water bottle, and any preferred basic first-aid supplies.
- Use luggage suitable for the transport booked. Small aircraft may have strict weight and bag-size limits; check directly with the airline or operator.
- Use neutral-colored clothing for some wildlife activities, but follow the specific guidance of the lodge or guide.

## Health, Entry, and Safety Checks

Entry permissions, electronic travel authorization rules, fees, passport validity requirements, and vaccination guidance can change. Check the current requirements with Kenya's official immigration authority and an appropriate travel-health professional before booking and again before departure.

Yellow fever documentation may be relevant depending on the traveler's itinerary and country of origin or transit. Requirements depend on current rules and individual circumstances; verify with official health guidance rather than relying on this guide.

Safety conditions vary by location and can change. Review your government's current travel advisory, follow local advice, use reputable guides and transport providers, and keep valuables secure. For remote travel, confirm communications coverage, emergency arrangements, and medical support with the operator.

This document is general travel information, not legal, medical, or security advice. It does not provide live prices, availability, weather, or entry rules.

## Budget Planning

Kenya can be planned at different budget levels, but total cost depends strongly on accommodation, park and conservancy fees, transport, season, and whether a safari uses private or shared vehicles.

- Lower-cost trips can use simpler accommodation, public or shared transport where practical, and fewer paid activities.
- Mid-range trips often combine comfortable lodges or hotels with a mix of road transfers and selected activities.
- Higher-cost safari trips may include private vehicles, premium lodges, internal flights, and conservancy experiences.
- Ask for an itemized quote. Confirm currency, taxes, park or conservation fees, meals, transfers, tips, cancellation terms, and any extra charges.
- Prices and fees change. Get current quotes directly from providers and official park or conservation authorities.

## Responsible Travel

Prefer operators that employ local guides, respect park rules, explain conservation fees, and describe how tourism supports nearby communities. Reduce single-use plastic where practical, conserve water, stay on designated paths, and avoid wildlife attractions that allow unsafe handling or inappropriate contact with wild animals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Kenya only a safari destination?

No. A trip can include cities, highlands, lakes, coast, historical sites, food, and cultural experiences as well as wildlife areas.

### Can I see the Great Migration on a specific date?

No date guarantees a sighting or river crossing. The movement depends on seasonal rainfall and grazing, and wildlife is unpredictable. Use recent local information and keep plans flexible.

### How many places should I include?

For a short trip, choose one main region. For a longer trip, combine a small number of regions and account for transfer time and rest.

### Do I need to arrange entry permission before travel?

Entry requirements can change and depend on nationality and itinerary. Check Kenya's official immigration information for the current process before purchasing non-refundable travel.

### Are park fees and game drives always included in lodge prices?

No. Inclusions vary by provider and package. Request an itemized confirmation before booking.
